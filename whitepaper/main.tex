\begin{abstract}
% TODO Week 4 Day 4 — finalize after results are in.
We replace the reactive EMA-smoothed supply-rate observation in a 4-factor
TOPSIS-style MCDM allocator across Aave V3 and Compound V3 USDC markets
with a 12-hour-ahead dual-branch DA-BiGRU-CNN forecast that decomposes the
supply rate into a deterministic kink component and a learned residual.
The strategy is implemented on the \texttt{fractal-defi} research substrate
and backtested over 18 months of historical mainnet data (Nov 2024 -- Apr 2026).
Compared to four baselines---buy-and-hold Aave V3, APY-only greedy, reactive
MCDM-with-EMA (ported from~\citep{solovev2026vault}), and a CIR-calibrated
classical forecast---the predictive variant achieves a Sharpe improvement
of \textbf{TODO} ($p = $ \textbf{TODO} via 1000-bootstrap of monthly ratios)
on the held-out test window (Jan -- Apr 2026). We additionally contribute
to the open-source ecosystem: a Compound V3 lending loader with uniform
utilization-field exposure across lending global states, and a
\texttt{BaseLendingAllocationStrategy} abstraction generalizing the
multi-protocol allocator pattern beyond this work.
\end{abstract}
