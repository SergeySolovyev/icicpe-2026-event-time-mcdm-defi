\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

This section reports the realized outcomes of the held-out test window
2026~Q1--Q2 (1~January~2026 -- 30~April~2026, plus the partial 720-hour
2026~Q2 anchor described in \cref{sec:empirical-spread}). All numbers
are end-to-end on the $\$1{,}000{,}000$~USDC notional with the gas-cost
and execution-bridge assumptions of \cref{sec:exec}, and are pulled
verbatim from \texttt{results/tables/main.csv},
\texttt{results/tables/ablations.csv}, and
\texttt{results/tables/h1\_significance.csv} via
\texttt{scripts/fill\_whitepaper\_results.py}.

\subsection{Headline: Predictive vs Reactive MCDM-EMA}
\label{sec:headline}

Over the four-month held-out test window the proposed predictive MCDM
allocator delivers a net APY of \PredictiveAPY{} against \EMAAPY{} for
the reactive MCDM-EMA baseline of \cite{solovev2026vault}, a Sharpe
ratio of \PredictiveSharpe{} against \EMASharpe{} for the baseline,
and an annualized Sharpe delta of \SharpeDelta{} in favor of the
predictive variant. The $1000$-replicate stationary bootstrap of
monthly Sharpe ratios (H1 test) returns a one-sided $p$-value of
\BootstrapPvalue{} with $95\%$ confidence interval
$[\BootstrapCILow{},\,\BootstrapCIHigh{}]$ on the Sharpe difference.
Whether this delta meets the pre-registered $0.2$--$0.5$ Sharpe
improvement threshold of H1 is read directly off these numbers; the
null hypothesis H0 (forecast adds no value over EMA on this horizon)
is rejected at the $5\%$ level iff the confidence interval excludes
zero.

\subsection{Regime-Conditional Breakdown}
\label{sec:regime-results}

The test window spans the second of the two empirically distinct
quarterly regimes documented in \cref{sec:empirical-spread}: 2026~Q1
(Compound-calm, $27.1\%$ Aave-pays-more share, $\sigma=0.57$\,pp) and
the partial 2026~Q2 (Aave-dominant, $61.3\%$ Aave-pays-more share,
$\sigma=3.61$\,pp). \Cref{tab:regime-results} reports the headline
metrics for the predictive variant and the EMA baseline separately
on each sub-window; we pre-register the expectation that the forecast
edge concentrates in 2026~Q2, where cross-protocol dispersion is
largest and the regime shift documented in \cref{sec:regime-empirical}
is in active progress.
