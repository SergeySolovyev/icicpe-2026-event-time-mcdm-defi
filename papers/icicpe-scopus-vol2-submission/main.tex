\begin{abstract}
We design an event-time, gas-aware multi-protocol allocator for USDC supply markets across the six largest Ethereum-L1 lending protocols (Aave V3, Compound V3, Spark, Morpho Blue, Euler V2, Fluid; $\sim$67\% of $\sim$\$54B TVL). A three-tier policy ladder is evaluated on every block: T1 gas-aware threshold, T2 OU optimal stopping with closed-form Bellman boundary, T3 Cox proportional-hazards on MacKenzie (2021) Table 3.2 F1/F3/F4 signal-class features trained with L\'opez de Prado AFML methodology (triple-barrier labels, purged $k$-fold CV with embargo, sample-uniqueness weighting). Over six non-overlapping three-month walk-forward windows (Nov 2024 -- Apr 2026), T3 beats passive holds of all six protocols on the 6-way active panel: $+2.88$\,pp vs Aave, $+2.70$\,pp vs Compound, $+1.85$\,pp vs Spark, $+2.65$\,pp vs Morpho, $+2.70$\,pp vs Fluid, $+1.53$\,pp vs Euler (paired-bootstrap $p<0.05$ on all six; $5$--$6$ of $6$ windows positive). The Cox model adds $+7.03$\,bp over T1 ($p=0.015$), closing pre-registered H1c. The Maker DSR one-hour delta and USDC peg deviation are the strongest non-fragmentation predictors. A production-grade Python agent (Flashbots private mempool, Prometheus observability) shares decision modules bit-identically with the backtest.
\end{abstract}
