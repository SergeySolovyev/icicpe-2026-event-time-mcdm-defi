\begin{abstract}
We design an event-time, gas-aware multi-protocol allocator for USDC supply markets across the six largest Ethereum-L1 lending protocols (Aave V3, Compound V3, Spark, Morpho Blue, Euler V2, Fluid; $\sim$67\% of $\sim$\$54B TVL).  A three-tier policy ladder is evaluated on every block: T1 gas-aware threshold, T2 Ornstein--Uhlenbeck optimal stopping with closed-form Bellman boundary, T3 Cox proportional-hazards on MacKenzie (2021) Table 3.2 F1 (lead-rate) and F3 (fragmentation) signal-class features, trained with L\'opez de Prado AFML methodology (triple-barrier labels, purged $k$-fold CV with embargo, sample-uniqueness weighting).  Over six non-overlapping three-month walk-forward windows (Nov 2024--Apr 2026), the active T1 policy outperforms each passive single-protocol hold by $+1.5$ to $+2.8$\,pp annualized ($p<10^{-4}$ on five of six contrasts; $p=0.026$ for Euler V2).  The F1$+$F3 sophisticated-retrain T3 adds $+7.03$\,bp over T1 (paired-bootstrap $p = 0.015$, $5$ of $6$ windows positive), closing pre-registered H1c.  F1's Maker DSR one-hour delta is the single non-fragmentation feature with materially positive out-of-fold C-index lift; F4 (USDC peg $+$ ETH/USD) contributes $\sim 0$\,pp and is reported as an honest negative finding.  A production-grade Python agent (Flashbots private mempool, Prometheus observability) shares decision modules bit-identically with the backtest.
\end{abstract}
