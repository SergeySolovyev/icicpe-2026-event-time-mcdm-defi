Short abstract for portal field:
This paper studies event-time, gas-aware allocation across Ethereum-L1 USDC lending protocols. Using a per-block panel for Aave V3, Morpho Blue, and Euler V2, with Compound V3, Spark, and Fluid as passive-hold benchmarks, we compare passive allocation, threshold, Ornstein-Uhlenbeck stopping, and Cox proportional-hazards decision policies. A closed-form gas-aware threshold rule outperforms passive single-protocol holds by 1.5-2.8 percentage points annualized across six non-overlapping Nov 2024-Apr 2026 walk-forward windows. A Cox hazards tier based on HFT-inspired lead-rate and fragmentation signals adds 7.03 basis points over the threshold baseline, with paired-bootstrap p = 0.015. The results suggest that the economically relevant edge in DeFi lending comes from inter-block microstructure and gas-adjusted switching costs rather than slower polling cadences. We also report a negative result for related-instrument signals and discuss limitations around active protocol coverage, gas measurement, mempool features, and prospective deployment validation.
