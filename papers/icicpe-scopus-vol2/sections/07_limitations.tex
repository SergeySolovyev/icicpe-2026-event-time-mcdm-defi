\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:limitations}

We disclose every methodological constraint affecting the headline
results so readers can scope conclusions appropriately and so that
reproductions can target the same envelope.

\textbf{(i) Walk-forward $N = 6$ windows.}  Per-window
$\Delta$APY standard errors are $\sim 5$--$10$\,bp; T3-over-T1 mean
$+7.03$\,bp clears one-sided $p<0.05$ with CI floor $+0.48$\,bp.
Overlapping 12-week windows with overlap-corrected bootstrap would
tighten the CI; deferred to the journal extension.

\textbf{(ii) F4 gas placeholder.}  The F4 \texttt{gas\_log10}
sub-signal uses a constant $25$\,gwei pending an Etherscan key
with sufficient quota; the zero-variance filter drops the column,
leaving F4 with only ETH/USD and USDC peg. F4 thus contributes
\emph{zero} OOF C-index above F3-only --- a methodological, not
empirical, finding. Real per-block gas re-run is queued.

\textbf{(iii) F2 mempool deferred.}  The MacKenzie F2
``order-book dynamics'' class requires paid Blocknative or
archived mempool ($\sim 30$~TB); not available in this study.

\textbf{(iv) Heterogeneous rate-refresh cadence.}  T1/T2/T3 actively
switch among all six protocols on the per-block grid, but the six
refresh their on-chain rates at different native cadences: Aave V3
($\sim 48$\,s) and Euler V2 ($\sim 6$\,min) are genuinely
sub-hourly; Spark and Compound V3 are sub-hourly via subgraph and
RPC sampling; Morpho Blue is $\sim$hourly; and Fluid updates only
$\sim$daily.  Switch decisions into the three slower venues act on a
forward-filled most-recent rate (the live-agent information set), so
we claim a per-block \emph{decision loop}, not per-block rate
\emph{resolution} for all six.  The three genuine sub-hourly venues
carry $\sim 63$--$64\%$ of held time (\Cref{tab:timeshare});
per-block fetchers for the slower three are the next-extension scope.

\textbf{(v) Capacity is analytic.}  The capacity sweep applies the
linearized Krause-2005 yield-impact rather than re-running the
replay engine at each position size; the time-share-weighted
average smooths the small-pool tail.

\textbf{(vi) Prospective deployment validation.}  The evaluation
reported here is retrospective and testnet/paper-trading only.
A prospective study is required before drawing conclusions about
execution quality or realized performance in production markets.
