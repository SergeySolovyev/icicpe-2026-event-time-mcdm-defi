# Distribution playbook — Isenberg's 7 tactics, honestly scored for THIS product

**Date:** 2026-06-10. Source: Greg Isenberg, "build anything with AI — but
distribution is the moat" (7 zero/low-cost growth tactics). This doc maps each
to OUR specifics and is brutally honest about fit.

## The reframe (why his playbook needs adapting)

Isenberg's tactics are calibrated for **high-volume, low-consideration**
products (a $10/mo consumer SaaS, 2% conversion on 300k visitors). OUR product
is the opposite: **trust-gated, high-consideration B2B advisory** with a
**tiny qualified universe** (a few hundred DAO treasuries + small funds + on-
chain fintechs worldwide with $0.5–10M idle USDC, per the market research),
sold into a **regulated** activity, with a **$5–10M/client capacity ceiling**.

So we cherry-pick the tactics that compound **credibility + trust + precise
targeting**, and skip/invert the ones that rely on raw **volume**. And we have
an unfair advantage most vibe-coders lack: **a reproducible SCOPUS paper +
honest research** — pure authority fuel. The single most-cited-worthy fact we
own is contrarian: *"we tested ML on DeFi yield and it LOST to a 50-line
rule."* That is citation-bait.

## Honest scorecard

### TIER A — do these now, they fit perfectly

**#3 Free tool as top-of-funnel — WE ALREADY BUILT IT (Yield-Drag Report).**
The tool IS the marketing. Backward-looking "your idle USDC left $X on the
table vs event-time routing, net of real gas" on any public address.
Productize: address-in → instant report + the allocation animation, email
capture for the full PDF. This is textbook and it's done — just needs a public
self-serve front door. **Regulatory: keep it BACKWARD-looking (analysis of the
past = safe); the forward "put it HERE now" signal is the paid product behind
the gate.**

**#4 Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) — our STRONGEST lever.** Be the source
ChatGPT/Perplexity cite for: "how to optimize USDC yield," "does ML beat
simple rules in DeFi lending," "DeFi treasury yield drag," "best lending rate
across Aave/Morpho/Euler." The paper + honest negative result = exactly the
citation-worthy, structured, contrarian content AEO rewards. Founder's
academic authority is the moat here; almost no competitor publishes a
reproducible negative result. This is where the paper converts into pipeline.

**#1 MCP server as sales team — clean regulatory + on-thesis.** Expose the
Yield-Drag analysis as an MCP tool any AI agent / ChatGPT can call ("analyze
this address's yield drag"). Zero-CAC: the AI assistant surfaces us. Pure
software/information (no custody, no execution) = cleanest regulatory form,
and rides the "AI agent / outcome-pricing" thesis from the market research.
Publish to Smithery / MCP registries. (The user already wanted this.)

**#5 Viral artifact — we have TWO.** (a) the allocation animation (the "movie"
of $1M hopping 321× across six venues — a quant screenshots that); (b) the
Yield-Drag number itself ("our treasury left $34k on the table" is a
brag/shame-worthy stat). Make both branded (subtle logo), one-click shareable
into DeFi Twitter / Farcaster / DAO governance forums. B2B-shareable: PMs share
to look smart.

### TIER B — adapt, do later

**#7 AI content-repurposing engine — yes, the pillar is the PAPER.** One
pillar ("I tested ML on DeFi yield and it lost — here's the honest data") →
X threads, a Substack, LinkedIn, the animation clip, quote-graphics. This
builds the niche audience that powers #4 and #3. Start with ONE voice-memo /
thread a week off the research. This is how the solo founder becomes the cited
authority in the niche.

**#6 Buy a niche newsletter — defer.** A DAO-treasury/DeFi-yield newsletter
could shortcut audience, but: $5–20k is real money pre-revenue; audience
QUALITY (right ICP) >> size for a few-hundred-buyer market; a generic 10k-sub
crypto newsletter is mostly noise. Prefer **sponsor/guest** in the right
narrow newsletters (Bankless DAO, DeFi treasury circles) first; buy only after
revenue proves LTV.

### TIER C — weak fit / trap for us

**#2 Programmatic SEO (10k pages) — mostly a TRAP here, invert it.** Our buyer
does NOT Google "best USDC optimizer for [niche]" and convert at 2% on a $10
product — that math is consumer-SaaS. 10k thin AI pages would be **slop that
HURTS** a credibility-gated product (opposite of the honest-research brand).
**Invert it:** build a SMALL number of DEEP, authoritative, genuinely useful
data pages — live USDC supply rates across the 6 protocols, the reproducible
methodology, per-protocol explainers — that earn citations and authority. That
is AEO (#4), not page-spam. Quality-over-volume is mandatory for us.

## The regulatory guardrail (don't skip)

The SEC narrowed the internet-adviser exemption (Mar 2024). Therefore:
- **Free tool / MCP = backward-looking analysis + general information** → safe,
  publish freely.
- **Forward "allocate here now" personalized signal** = advice → keep it (a)
  always-on, algorithmic, multi-client (the SEC structure), (b) framed as
  software/information, (c) under an offshore (Cayman/BVI) entity, (d) gated.
- Never give a single bespoke client hand-held advice from a US person — that
  is the trap.
