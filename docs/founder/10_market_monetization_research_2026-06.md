# Market, monetization & competitor research — can we sell this, for how much?

**Date:** 2026-06-10. **Method:** deep-research harness (104 agents, 4.3M
tokens, ~26 min): 5 search angles → ~15 sources fetched → 3-vote adversarial
verification per claim (2/3 refutes kills it) → cited synthesis. All 7
findings below are `high` confidence, 3-0 verified.

## Blunt verdict

**This is a credibility asset + a lifestyle/consulting business, NOT a
venture-scale company.** The ~$5–10M-per-client capacity ceiling on a
thin-venue edge structurally caps a performance-fee model. The market is real
and large, but the high-AUM ground is already held by custodial curators
(Gauntlet ~$2B, Steakhouse $1.29B) and a non-custodial benchmark (Karpatkey,
$1.8B AUA, raised $7M Oct 2024). **Highest-leverage move: ship the free
Yield-Drag Report wedge at scale → convert measured, attributable drag into
the first outcome-based paid engagement**, structured offshore/software-only
to sidestep the US adviser-registration trap.

## The 7 verified findings

1. **Market is large, fast-growing, concentrated.** DeFi "curator" category
   $300M → $7B TVL in <1 yr (~2,200%); top 5 = ~43%. Gauntlet ~$2B (27.6%),
   Steakhouse $1.29B (17.8%), MEV Capital $915M, K3 $478M (arXiv 2512.11976;
   Chorus One, Dec 2025). **Caveat:** these are *custodial discretionary*
   MetaMorpho vaults — adjacent to, not identical to, our non-custodial router.

2. **Prevailing fee model:** performance fee on yield **5–10%** (Morpho
   contract cap **50%**), optional management fee **≤5%/yr**. Yearn: 5–10% on
   Morpho deposits, 10% on V3 multi-strategy. Re7 captures ~19% of gross yield
   (~$1.88M revenue). **Flagship vaults competing to 0% perf fee → real
   fee-compression risk.** (Morpho docs; Yearn docs; Chorus One; DeFiLlama.)

3. **Fee-capture is thin for newcomers.** Re7 ~16%, Block Analitica ~14%, K3
   ~10%, MEV ~9%, **Steakhouse & Yearn <3%** (near cost-recovery to win AUM).
   A new entrant cannot expect to retain a large fraction of gross yield.
   (arXiv 2512.11976; DeFiLlama.)

4. **AI outcome-based pricing is the macro tailwind.** Bessemer: "AI will
   monetize outcomes." a16z: "software is becoming labor; per-seat is no
   longer the atomic unit" (Decagon prices per-resolution). Sequoia/Bret
   Taylor (Sierra): "pay for a job well done." **Fits charging per captured
   bp net of gas perfectly.** Nuance: market consolidated on **HYBRID** (base
   fee + outcome charge); pure outcome pricing hits attribution disputes
   (Cursor backlash, Jun 2025). (Bessemer; a16z; Sequoia.)

5. **Non-custodial DAO-treasury IS fundable — but the ceiling is set by
   Karpatkey.** kpk: $1.8B non-custodial AUA in client-owned Safes (ENS, Aave,
   Lido, Balancer, CoW, Gnosis), 10,000+ tx no incident, raised $7M Oct 2024
   (AppWorks, Wintermute, Lubin, Kulechov, Martinelli). This is both the
   proof-of-concept AND the competitive ceiling. (Note: Gnosis moving to
   terminate kpk, Nov 2025.) (kpk.io; TheBlock.)

6. **Sizing inputs (verified) are huge — but the deployable slice is small.**
   DeFi lending TVL **$64.06B** end-2025 (+33% YTD); stablecoin cap **$305B**
   (USDT+USDC ~85%, Ethereum ~55%); Aave $12.78B, Spark $7.45B, Morpho $6.95B
   protocol-wide. **Critical:** the edge-carrying USDC venues are thin
   (Spark ~$29M, Euler ~$16M USDC supply) → physically caps the strategy at
   ~$5–10M/client. Idle-USDC pool is huge; deployable-per-client is tiny.
   (DL News State of DeFi 2025, Dec 23 2025.)

7. **US regulatory trap.** SEC narrowed the "internet investment adviser
   exemption" (Rule 203A-2(e), adopted Mar 27 2024, compliance Mar 31 2025):
   bespoke one-or-few-client advisory does NOT qualify; you must run an
   always-on interactive site serving >1 client with advice "generated by"
   the algorithm. → Structure as **offshore (Cayman/BVI) advisory,
   software-only, or an always-on multi-client web app** — not human-bespoke
   US advisory. (SEC press release 2024-42; K&L Gates; Goodwin; Kitces.)

## Revenue math — bottom-up, brutally honest (my derivation from the inputs)

Per-client economics (avg deployable ~$5M, full-strategy edge):
- Perf fee on **outperformance vs passive** (our honest framing): edge
  +2.1–3.7 pp on $5M = **$105k–$185k outperformance/yr** → at a 15–20% perf
  fee = **~$16k–$37k per client/yr**.
- Or perf fee on **total yield** (curator standard): ~5–8% on $5M =
  $250k–$400k → at 5–10% = **~$12k–$40k per client/yr**.
- Both land at **~$15k–$35k per client per year**. Call base case **~$20k**.

Scenarios (solo → small team):

| | Clients | Avg size | Rev/client | **Annual revenue** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Bear (yr 1)** | 3 | $5M | ~$15k | **~$45k** (consulting income) |
| **Base (yr 2)** | 10 | $5M | ~$20k | **~$200k** (solid solo) |
| **Bull (yr 3)** | 25–30 | $5–7M | ~$25k | **~$0.6–0.75M** (small team) |
| **Practical ceiling** | 40–50 | $5–10M | ~$25k | **~$1.0–1.25M/yr** |

The hard ceiling: high-touch trust + $5–10M/client means you can't have 1,000
clients. ~40–50 clients ≈ $200–500M AUA ≈ **$1–1.25M/yr revenue** — an
excellent solo/small-team business, **not** a $100M-revenue venture path. For
scale calibration: even category-leader **Karpatkey at $1.8B AUA** is likely
only a **single-digit-$M/yr** revenue business in this exact niche.

## To break the ceiling (what venture-scale would require)

- **L2 expansion** (Base, Arbitrum) — deeper pools, more clients, more venues.
- **Deep-venue-only version** for $25M+ clients at a lower (still positive) edge.
- **Multi-strategy** beyond lending (LP, basis, RWA) — more deployable depth.
- **Become a curator** (custodial vaults) — capture a % of much larger AUM —
  but this inherits the custody risk + regulatory burden the no-token,
  non-custodial honesty thesis was built to avoid. **Strategic fork:** stay
  honest-and-small, or take-custody-and-scale.

## Where the white space genuinely is

Non-custodial + transparent closed-form + cross-protocol + *published honest
track record* is real differentiation vs custodial black-box curators
(Gauntlet/Steakhouse) and even vs Karpatkey (discretionary, not a transparent
rule). The honesty that caps scale is the same honesty that wins trust-gated
buyers. The wedge — a free, reproducible "here's what passive cost you on your
public address" report — is the cheapest trust manufacture available and the
right first move.
