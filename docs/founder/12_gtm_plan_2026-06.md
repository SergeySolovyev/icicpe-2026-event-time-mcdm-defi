## What this product is

**One-liner:** A non-custodial "idle-USDC rate router" — it checks, per
Ethereum block, which of the six biggest lending venues pays the most on a
treasury's idle dollars and moves the money to the winner **from the client's
own Safe (we never hold funds)** — and we publish exactly how much it earns
net of gas, *including the times a fancier AI model did NOT help*.

**Category to own:** `non-custodial stablecoin rate router` (plain English:
"idle-USDC yield router"). Do **not** compete in the crowded custodial
"curator/vault" category. No incumbent owns this exact phrase.

**Name candidates:** **DriftZero** (encodes the enemy = yield drag + the moat =
zero research-to-prod drift) — lead pick; or **BlockRoute** (per-block routing).
Avoid "Idle*" (Idle Finance exists).

**Sellable TODAY (the wedge):** the backward-looking **Yield-Drag Report**
("here's what passive idle USDC cost you, net of real gas") + the 321-switch
allocation **animation** + the reproducible **SCOPUS paper**. The live agent
(Sepolia, 2/6 adapters, unaudited) is **NOT** the lead — it's what you sell
*after* the report books the meeting.

**The moat is trust, not the model:** (1) we published that **ML LOST** to a
50-line rule (−5.97 bp, 0/5 windows) — a reproducible negative result no
competitor ships; (2) reproducible research + per-block 6-protocol panel;
(3) non-custodial by construction; (4) zero research↔prod drift.

**Anti-positioning (never claim):** not "AI-powered" (our hook is the
opposite), not custodial/a vault/a curator, not "beats the market" (we beat
*passive idle USDC*, a drag-recovery claim), no token, and the free tool is
never forward "put it here now" advice.

## Pricing & the first dollar (red-team-corrected)

- **Do NOT price the first report at $500** (critic's #2 fix): it's 60–70× below
  the ~$15–35k/client/yr real business and reads as a favor/hobby — a "yes"
  proves nothing. **Rung 1 = either FREE-with-a-signed-upsell-intent, or a
  $2.5–5k flat "Treasury Yield-Analytics Report."**
- **Offer ladder:** free Yield-Drag Report (lead) → $2.5–5k flat backward-looking
  report + 90-day monitoring → (post-counsel, post-mainnet) the live signal at
  **base fee + capped outcome fee on net-of-gas outperformance**, benchmark
  frozen at period start, client-reproducible. Never 1% of the treasury.
- **Regulatory gate (critic's #8 — hard ordering fix):** a paid *bespoke* report
  on a client's real treasury is closer to the adviser line than a free general
  tool. So: **counsel-blessed ToS + US LLC + OFAC wallet-screening must be in
  hand BEFORE the first invoice** (not Week 8). The forward signal + outcome fee
  stay gated behind a written counsel opinion (adviser-status + USDC-not-a-
  security) and an offshore (Cayman/BVI) structure. Free reports (no money
  changes hands) are the clearly-safe research side and can start immediately.

## Must-fix in the DELIVERABLE before any report ships (critic's gold)

These are real flaws a quant buyer spots in 60 seconds — fixing them is the
cheapest, highest-leverage work, and a blown first call burns a scarce warm intro:
1. **Benchmark vs the buyer's REAL alternative, not a strawman.** The sample
   compares to passive single-protocol Aave; the honest baseline is a
   **Morpho/Eco auto-router**. Compute and show that delta.
2. **Show the size-haircut.** A $5M position routes 49% into Euler ($16M TVL) =
   ~31% of the whole pool. Show edge at **$0.5M / $2M / $5M** so the headline
   isn't trivially falsifiable by anyone who knows Euler's TVL.
3. **Reconcile the gas number.** $99 for 378 L1 rebalances ($0.26 each) is real
   *at the 0.3-gwei test window* but looks impossibly low — disclose it at
   multiple gas levels (e.g. ~$6.6k at 25 gwei, from our own sweep).
4. **Fix the contradictory copy.** The sample's "binding edge holds 6/6
   walk-forward (p<0.001)" sits two lines above "ML lost" — reads as
   over-claim/confusion to a quant. That 6/6 is the *rule's* edge; separate it
   cleanly from the ML-negative, and pick ONE window-matched headline
   (+2.1pp/4-mo OR +3.7–3.9pp/walk-forward — not both as "headline").
5. **Retarget the RFP.** The GnosisDAO RFP is **CLOSED** (Noca/Noctua won,
   Jan–Feb 2026). Use **Scroll's open RFP** + successors as the "Partial Scope
   tooling/reporting" pipeline.
